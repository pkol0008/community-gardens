Australia · Community · Gardening

Growing Together

A visual exploration of Australia's community gardens: where they are, how large they are, when they were established, and how they are recorded as being accessed.

This project uses a community garden directory as its primary dataset. The records provide a useful view of community gardening across Australia, but they may not represent every garden operating today.

01 / Where

Where are the gardens?

We begin with the geographic distribution of community garden records. State shading shows how records are distributed between regions, while individual circles identify garden locations and their recorded sizes.

02 / Access

Are the gardens open to the public?

Location alone does not tellhow these spaces can be used. The are several forms of public access, ranging from gardens open to the general public to spaces reserved for members or clients.

03 / Garden settings

What kinds of garden spaces are represented?

Most records describe general community gardens, while the directory also contains school gardens, city farms, verge gardens and a small number of other shared growing spaces.

04 / Change over time

Did garden size change across establishment eras?

These box plots compare recorded garden areas across broad establishment periods. The logarithmic scale keeps unusually large sites from compressing the rest of the distributions.

05 / Size

How large are the gardens?

Grouping recorded garden areas into ranges reveals the overall size distribution and shows how common smaller and larger sites are.

06 / Regional size

Which states have larger gardens?

Using the median reduces the influence of extremely large sites and makes typical recorded garden size easier to compare between regions.

07 / Size distributions

How do garden sizes differ between states?

Medians can hide variation. These ridgeline distributions show the broader spread of recorded garden sizes across states and territories.

08 / Access & setting

Does public access vary by garden setting?

This view compares the proportion of recorded access categories within each kind of garden space represented in the directory.

09 / Regional patterns

How do garden settings vary across states?

This heatmap compares the number of records for each garden setting across states and territories.

10 / Access composition

How are public-access patterns distributed across states?

Each state's width reflects its share of directory records, while the coloured divisions show the public-access mix within that state.

11 / Connections

How do region, garden setting and public access connect?

Records move from broad region, through garden setting, to their recorded public-access category. Thicker connections represent more records.

12 / Population context

Where are garden records most concentrated relative to population?

This map compares directory counts with 2021 Census population, showing garden records per 100,000 residents.

Conclusion

A varied community garden landscape

Taken together, the records show considerable variation in the location, size, age and recorded accessibility of community garden spaces across Australia.

These findings describe patterns within the available datasets rather than a complete census of every community garden currently operating in Australia.
